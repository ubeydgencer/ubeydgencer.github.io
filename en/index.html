Summary1.0

I have been around computers, networks and cameras since 2013. I started as a computer technician at Motor Aşin Otomotiv, then founded Qufe Silver and ran it for close to four years. Since 2020 I have worked on IT and business development at Açsa Otomotiv, building and running the company’s internal tools: warehouse dispatch tracking, annual leave calculation, fleet compliance, product sites.

I hold degrees in Computer Engineering from Fırat University and Management Information Systems from Anadolu University. I volunteer with AFAD, Türkiye’s disaster and emergency management authority, and with TRAC, the Turkish radio amateurs’ society. Technology, entrepreneurship, design and photography all have my attention.

This site is arranged like a manual: section 1 is who I am, section 2 is what I have shipped, section 3 is what I use.
