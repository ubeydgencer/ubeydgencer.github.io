Fixing TV+ freezes behind GL.iNet with VLANs

Network note · MT3000 · 802.1Q VLAN

One stream, two networks§1

I wanted my home devices behind a GL.iNet MT3000, but Turkcell TV+ kept freezing on that connection. The working solution was to use two managed switches to carry a separate modem network over the existing cable to the TV box.

These are my notes from 3 November 2026. Freezing stopped after I completed the VLAN connection. I did not establish the exact mechanism that caused the problem on the path through the MT3000.

The original path§2

The connection ran from a ZTE H3600 V9 modem through the MT3000, then a TL-SG105PE and a TL-SG105E. There was also an older switch before the TV+ box. GL.iNet handed out 192.168.8.x addresses; the modem used 192.168.1.x.

The modem and TV box were in different places. Running a new direct cable all the way to the TV box was impractical, so I needed to use the existing route.

Settings I tried§3

I restarted the modem and TV box, disabled Network Acceleration on the MT3000, and removed the last old switch. Freezing continued. I then enabled IGMP Snooping, first with version 3 and then version 2. Neither test fixed it.

The router reported 1000 Mbps full duplex on both WAN and LAN. No VPN tunnel was configured. Negotiated speed does not prove the absence of packet loss, but there was no obvious speed downgrade in the panel.

I did not capture packets to establish how the stream was transported. Multicast handling was a possibility I tested, rather than a confirmed diagnosis. These results did not prove a defective router or a particular firmware bug.

The useful comparison§4

The modem and MT3000 were next to each other. I temporarily moved the cable feeding the switch chain from the router to a free modem LAN port. With the old final switch also removed, freezing stopped.

I put the MT3000 back into the path while leaving the old switch out. After the TV box restarted, freezing returned. This narrowed the investigation to the path through the MT3000; it did not distinguish the precise feature, cable or port responsible.

I wanted to keep GL.iNet for the home network. The compromise was an exception for TV+: other devices would remain behind the router, while TV+ would get a separate path to the modem.

A separate path over the same cable§5

Both switches supported 802.1Q configuration. I kept the home network on VLAN 1 and created VLAN 20 for TV+. 20 is a locally chosen VLAN ID for this setup. It is not a service VLAN ID supplied by Turkcell.

The modem LAN1-to-MT3000-WAN cable stayed in place. I added a cable from a spare modem LAN port to port 2 of the first switch. The existing inter-switch cable then carried the home and modem networks in separate VLANs.

VLAN 20 travels tagged between switches and untagged on the modem and TV+ ports. PVID 20 assigns untagged incoming traffic on those end ports to the TV+ VLAN. The home network remains untagged VLAN 1 on the inter-switch link.

First switch: TL-SG105PE§6

Port 2 is dedicated to the added modem connection. GL.iNet, Deco and Hue stay on VLAN 1.

Second switch: TL-SG105E§7

I removed the TV+ port, port 2, from VLAN 1 and made it a member of VLAN 20 only. The Mac mini, Deco X20 and Home Assistant remain on the home network.

I enabled 802.1Q on both switches, saved the VLAN memberships and set port 2 on each switch to PVID 20. I connected the additional modem cable only after that separation was configured. The modem and GL.iNet DHCP servers needed to stay in separate broadcast domains.

What worked§8

After connecting the cable and restarting TV+, the stream ran without freezing. TV+ now reaches the modem through the switches and bypasses GL.iNet routing and filtering. Other devices still use GL.iNet.

The expected addressing is 192.168.1.x for TV+ and 192.168.8.x for home devices. I did not independently verify the new TV+ lease in the modem panel: its session had expired. The observed result was that freezing stopped after the connection was completed, rather than a long-term performance measurement.

Network Acceleration remained disabled and IGMP Snooping remained enabled with version 2. Those settings had not fixed the problem on their own. Since the TV+ path now bypasses router forwarding, I do not credit them for the result.

The next expansion§9

I have ordered a TL-SG108E to add more ports and plan to move the TL-SG105E to the TV unit. TV+ will stay on the modem VLAN, with the PS4, Apple TV and Philips TV on the home network. That expansion has not been implemented; the tables above document the working two-switch setup.

The most useful step was the comparison test. Temporarily bypassing the router identified the path associated with freezing. VLANs then let me take only TV+ off that path while keeping the existing cable.
